About me

I'm studying Information Technology with a cybersecurity focus, and that's the area I'm most interested in. A lot of what I've learned so far has come from actually working through projects, labs, and problems instead of just reading about them.

I learn better by doing it. That could be working inside a virtual machine, figuring out a SQL query, troubleshooting something that isn't working, or testing how an AI assistant uses its own source information.

Right now I've been working with Windows and Linux security labs, databases, networking, and AI projects. One of the bigger ones was the RAG assistant I built using Python and Google Colab.

I'm still building my experience and figuring out exactly where I want to land in IT, but cybersecurity, systems, and the practical side of AI are the areas I keep coming back to.
